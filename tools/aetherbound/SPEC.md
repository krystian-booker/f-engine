# Aetherbound: Prologue — asset specification

The demo in `samples/aetherbound` is a ten-minute vertical slice: the last
battle of a war, at an Aetherite extraction fortress, at night. Four heroes
cross a shattered bridge, fight through a burning courtyard, wake an ancient
war machine (the Aetherbound Colossus) in the chamber under the central
tower, and one of them, Rowan, burns himself out to stop it. The last scene is
the same chamber in ruins before dawn.

Everything here is original and generated by scripts in this folder, so it can
be regenerated and carries no third-party licence. Do not download art, music
or samples. Do not imitate the characters, art or music of an existing game.

## Look

"HD-2D": pixel-art character sprites standing in a 3D world that is lit and
post-processed like a modern game (HDR, bloom, depth of field, fog, dynamic
point lights, particles). The world reads as hand-made miniature dioramas:
chunky, readable shapes, pixel-art textures (nearest filtering, 16 texels per
world unit on walls and floors, 32 on small props), strong light direction and
rich colour, never flat or grey.

Palette of this night:

| What | Colour |
| --- | --- |
| Stone, in shadow | deep indigo-charcoal `#1b1d2e` to `#34364d` |
| Stone, lit by fire | warm umber `#7a5a45` to `#c98b5a` |
| Fire, embers | `#ff7a1e` core `#ffd27a`, HDR (emissive 3-6) |
| Aetherite (crystal, conduits, magic) | violet `#9b5cff`, core `#e2ccff`, HDR (emissive 4-8); shadows magenta-indigo `#3a1a66` |
| Moonlit haze, sky | `#0c1024` to `#27305a` |
| Old metal (Colossus, machinery) | dark bronze `#4a3b2c`, verdigris `#3f6b5e`, gold inlay `#c9a24a` |
| Cael | navy coat, gold trim, short royal-blue cape, sandy hair |
| Rowan | charcoal armour, crimson sash and half-cloak, black hair |
| Lyra | cream long coat, teal trim, auburn hair tied back, staff with a small crystal |
| Darius | steel-grey plate, ochre tabard, grey-streaked hair and beard, round shield and axe |
| Fortress guards | rust-red and black plate, closed helms |

World scale: 1 unit is a grid cell. A hero is about 1.5 units tall (48 px at
32 px per unit). +x is east, +z is south, +y is up. The default field camera
looks north (yaw 0) from about 12 units south of and 6 above the party
(pitch 25-30°, fov 30°), so *nothing taller than about 2 units may stand
within 14 units south of where the party walks*, unless it is at least 6 units
to the east or west of the path. Tall things belong north of the action,
where they are the backdrop.

## Rules for everyone

- Work only in your own folders (listed per section) and do not touch
  `engine/`, `samples/starfall`, other sections' folders, or git (no commits).
  Do not rebuild the engine (`build/fengine` and `build/fengine_headless` are
  ready and shared).
- Generators live in `tools/aetherbound/<section>/` and write into
  `samples/aetherbound/...`. They must run again from scratch with
  `/tmp/claude-0/bl/venv/bin/python <script>` (Python 3.13 with `bpy` 5.3,
  `numpy`, `pillow`; add what you need with that venv's `pip`). Blender runs
  headless with Cycles on the CPU (`scene.render.engine = 'CYCLES'`, 4 cores);
  EEVEE and Workbench need a GPU and are not available.
- Look at what you make. `python3 tools/aetherbound/shot.py shot.json --out
  <folder>` renders anything in the real engine (see the docstring at its
  top); read the PNGs and keep improving until it would not look out of place
  in a commercial HD-2D game. The engine's formats are in `docs/authoring.md`
  (maps, sprites, models, lights, emitters, environment) and
  `docs/architecture.md` (rendering).
- `./build/fengine_headless --check samples/aetherbound` must report 0
  problems when you are done.
- Everything you generate follows its kit's guide and passes its gate: models
  `tools/modelkit/README.md`, their animations `tools/animkit/README.md`,
  textures `tools/texturekit/README.md`, sprite sheets
  `tools/spritekit/README.md`. Build with the kit's helpers, list the asset in
  `tools/aetherbound/<models|animations|textures|sprites>.json` with its
  profile, and leave `python3 tools/<kit>/validate.py --manifest
  tools/aetherbound/<manifest> --engine build/fengine_headless` passing, with
  the review sheet looked at and signed.
- Scratch files go in your own folder under
  `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-f-engine/c427e0fc-76b4-5426-b107-9b52cd2adb5c/scratchpad/`.
- Finish with a short report: files written, how to regenerate, the names and
  timings the game code needs (clips, animations, points), anything that
  deviates from this spec and why.

## Sprites — `tools/aetherbound/sprites/` → `samples/aetherbound/sprites/`

Six characters on one rig: `cael`, `rowan`, `lyra`, `darius`, `guard`
(sword), `lancer` (spear). Each is `<name>.png` + `<name>.anim.json`
(`pixels_per_unit` 32, `pivot` [0.5, 1]), frames 64 × 64 px with the feet on
the bottom centre and the figure about 46-50 px tall (chibi proportions,
about 2.5 heads; the head, hair and weapon must read at a glance). Pixel art:
hard edges, no anti-aliasing against the background (alpha is 0 or 255), a
dark 1 px outline, 3-4 tones per material, a limited palette. A normal map
(`<name>_n.png`, named by `normal` in the anim.json) is welcome if it makes
firelight move across them.

Every clip has 4 directions, rows in the order south (facing the viewer),
west, north, east:

| Clip | Frames | Loop | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `idle` | 4 × 0.30 s | yes | standing, breathing |
| `walk` | 6 × 0.10 s | yes | `event: "step"` on the two contact frames |
| `run` | 6 × 0.08 s | yes | leaning forward; `step` events |
| `ready` | 4 × 0.22 s | yes | battle stance, weapon drawn |
| `attack` | 6: 0.10, 0.08, 0.06, 0.10, 0.12, 0.14 | no | wind-up, swing; `event: "hit"` on frame 3 |
| `cast` | 4 × 0.16 s | yes | weapon or hand raised, channeling |
| `guard` | 2 × 0.4 s | yes | braced behind weapon or shield |
| `hurt` | 2: 0.12, 0.28 | no | recoil |
| `critical` | 2 × 0.5 s | yes | down on one knee, panting |
| `ko` | 1 | no | lying on the ground |
| `victory` | 2 × 0.4 s | yes | weapon raised |
| `kneel` | 1 | no | one knee, head bowed |
| `thrown` | 1 | no | hurled backwards through the air, limbs trailing |
| `rise` | 3 × 0.35 s | no | from lying, to one knee, to standing |
| `reach` | 1 | no | leaning forward, one arm stretched out |
| `raise` | 2 × 0.35 s | yes | one hand raised high, palm open |
| `shield` | 1 | no | forearm across the face, braced against a blast |
| `look_up` | 1 | no | standing, head tilted to the sky |
| `hold` | 1 | no | kneeling, cradling something in both hands, head bowed |

Also:

- `rowan_bound.anim.json`: Rowan's sheet again with an `emissive` image
  (`rowan_glow.png`): violet veins of light up the arms, neck and face that
  the game switches to when his secret comes out. Same layout as `rowan.png`.
- `sword_broken.png` + `.anim.json`: Rowan's longsword broken in two, lying
  on the ground, scorched (one 48 × 24 frame, clip `idle`), drawn flat.
- `sword_half.png` + `.anim.json`: one half of it, upright, for when Cael
  holds it (24 × 32).

## Colossus and crystal — `tools/aetherbound/colossus/` → `samples/aetherbound/models/`

`models/colossus.glb`: the Aetherbound Colossus, an ancient armoured war
machine, 7 units tall standing (origin between its feet on the ground, +Y up,
facing +Z at rest). Hunched and top-heavy: huge forearms and fists, a small
low head with two eye slits, layered plates of dark bronze and verdigris with
gold inlay lines, cables at the joints. A crystalline spire of violet
Aetherite is driven through its chest from high behind to low in front,
about 2.5 units out of its back and 1.5 out of its front; the wound glows.
Emissive parts (use `KHR_materials_emissive_strength`): the spire, the chest
core, the eye slits. The eyes are separate pieces whose bones scale them from
0 (dark) to 1 (lit), so an animation can light them.

One skin, at most 4 joints per vertex (rigid weighting is fine), at most
15,000 vertices (the engine skins on the CPU), textures embedded and at most
1024², at most 5 materials. Animations (glTF, by name):

| Name | Seconds | Loop | What |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `dormant` | 4 | yes | down on one knee, slumped, fists on the floor, eyes dark; barely moving |
| `awaken` | 5 | no | eyes light at 0.6 s; head lifts; it pushes itself up to standing and flexes; ends in the `idle` pose |
| `idle` | 3 | yes | standing, heavy slow sway |
| `slam` | 1.8 | no | right fist raised and smashed down in front; impact at 0.9 s |
| `sweep` | 2.0 | no | left arm swept wide across the front; impact at 1.0 s |
| `pulse` | 2.6 | no | rears back, arms spread, chest thrust out; releases at 1.6 s |
| `hit` | 0.6 | no | flinch |
| `stagger` | 2.5 | no | reels two steps back, drops to one knee and holds there |
| `overload_enter` | 3 | no | from the end of `stagger`: rises, arms spreading, head thrown back |
| `overload` | 2 | yes | arms wide, head back, trembling; the spire's fragments shiver |
| `sunder` | 6 | no | the spire tears apart: its fragments separate and drift 3-4 units up and outwards while the body arches and its hands claw at the wound; holds the last pose |

Also write `models/colossus.json`: positions in model space (at the `idle`
pose unless said) of `eye_left`, `eye_right`, `core` (the wound), `spire_tip`
(the high end), `fist_slam` (where the fist lands), `sweep_centre`, `head`,
plus `height`, and the yaw (degrees, as `world:spawn{yaw = }` takes it) that
turns the model to face +x and the one that faces -x.

`models/aether_crystal.glb`: the fortress's great Aetherite crystal that
floats above the central tower: a cluster of long double-terminated crystals,
14 units tall, the same violet material as the spire, with brighter veins
inside. One animation `idle` (20 s loop): a slow turn and bob. Up to 3,000
vertices.

`models/aether_shard.glb`: one small loose shard (0.5 unit), for debris.

## Fortress — `tools/aetherbound/fortress/` → `samples/aetherbound/maps/fortress/`

One map, 32 cells wide (x) and 72 deep (z), walked from south to north. All
of it burns: fires, embers, smoke, heat haze, bodies of guards, broken siege
gear, banners. In the far north stands the central tower with the great
crystal floating over it (the game spawns the crystal model at the point
`crystal`; do not bake it into the geometry).

- **Bridge**: deck x 13..18, z 50..69, height 0, over a chasm lost in fog,
  with parapets, broken in places. Its south end (z 70, 71) is torn away:
  jagged edge, nothing beyond. The opening shot of the game is here: the four
  heroes stand at z 62..65 and look north along the bridge at the gate, the
  walls, the tower and the crystal, with fire on the skyline. Make that view
  a painting.
- **Gate**: z 46..49, a passage x 14..17 through a gatehouse that has been
  blown open (its top is gone, so the camera sees over it; flanking towers
  may stand tall to the sides).
- **Courtyard**: x 3..28, z 20..45, height 0. Wreckage forces a winding path
  at least 3 cells wide: from the gate north, then *west* (first fight:
  enemies west of the party), north along the west side, then *east* along
  z 34..37 (second fight: enemies east of the party), north on the east side,
  and back west to the foot of the stairs.
- **Grand stair**: x 13..18, z 14..22, climbing north from height 0 to 3
  (ramps). **Landing**: x 11..20, z 8..13, height 3, before the tower's great
  door (x 14..17 at z 8).
- **Tower**: north of the landing, 40 units tall, the backdrop of the whole
  walk.

Points (position x, z; facing): `start` (16, 64; north), `cael` (15.5, 63.5),
`rowan` (16.6, 62.4), `lyra` (14.6, 64.6), `darius` (17.2, 65.0), all facing
north; `gate` (16, 49.5); `enc1_centre` (10.5, 42.5), `enc1_party1..4`
(x 13.0-14.2, z 41-44, staggered, facing west), `enc1_enemy1..2` (x 7.2-7.8,
z 41.8 and 43.4, facing east); `enc2_centre` (17.5, 35.5), `enc2_party1..4`
(x 14.0-15.2, z 34-37, facing east), `enc2_enemy1..3` (x 20.4-21.4, z 34.2,
35.6, 37.0, facing west); `stairs` (16, 22.5); `door` (16, 9.5; north);
`crystal` (16, 4, y 46). Zones (type `event`, boxes): `enc1` x 9..15,
z 40..45; `enc2` x 12..17, z 33..38; `to_chamber` x 13..19, z 8..10. Every
point and zone is on walkable ground, and `start` connects to `door`.

`props`: `{"music": "fortress", "camera": {"distance": 13, "pitch": 27, "fov": 30}}`.

## Chamber and ruins — `tools/aetherbound/chamber/` → `samples/aetherbound/maps/chamber/`, `maps/ruins/`

Two maps on one footprint (32 × 32 cells), built by one generator: the
chamber under the tower as the heroes find it, and the same place after the
blast.

**`chamber`**: a round hall, radius 13 around (16, 16), floor height 0. The
heroes come in from the east (door at x 29..31, z 15..17, a short passage
x 27..31, z 14..18). The Colossus kneels on a low dais (radius 3.5, not
walkable) at (11.5, 16), facing east; the game spawns it at the point `boss`.
Conduits of glowing Aetherite run across the floor into the dais, and the
north wall is a cliff of old machinery, pipes and pumps feeding them. Pillars
ring the hall, braziers burn, chains hang. There is no roof to see: above
the walls the tower's shaft goes up into darkness. The camera looks north, so
the south wall is absent or knee-high. The whole boss battle and the death
scene play here, seen from the south at pitch 18-25°, distance 20-26: the
Colossus (7 units tall) left of centre, the heroes on the right. That frame
must look magnificent.

Points: `start` (28.5, 16; west), `boss` (11.5, 16; east), `centre`
(16, 16), `intro1..4` (x 23-24.5, z 14.5-17.5; west), `party1` (20.5, 13.6),
`party2` (21.3, 15.2), `party3` (20.7, 16.8), `party4` (21.5, 18.4) (west),
`thrown1..4` (x 24.5-25.5, the same z; west), `rowan_walk` (15.5, 16; west),
`cael_stop` (18.5, 16; west).
`props`: `{"music": "colossus", "camera": {"distance": 22, "pitch": 22, "fov": 30}}`.

**`ruins`**: before dawn. The Colossus and the tower are gone: the north
wall is a slope of rubble with broken stumps of the tower still standing
behind it (the last shot pans up past them into the sky), the dais is a
scorched, cracked crater, the conduits are dark and split. Open sky: deep
blue-violet, a paler band at the horizon, thin smoke; no star (the game adds
one). Violet motes drift down through the whole space like snow, slowly.
Cold and quiet: almost no fire left, a little moonlight, faint violet
afterglow in the crater.

Points: as the chamber's, plus `cael_wake` (22.5, 17.5), `darius_wake`
(24.5, 16), `lyra_kneel` (13.2, 15.4; south-west), `sword` (12.5, 16),
`cael_run_end` (14.5, 16.6; west).
`props`: `{"music": "remains", "camera": {"distance": 20, "pitch": 24, "fov": 30}}`.

## Audio — `tools/aetherbound/audio/` → `samples/aetherbound/audio/`, `samples/aetherbound/data/audio.json`

Synthesised in Python (numpy/scipy; write Ogg Vorbis with `soundfile`),
44.1 kHz. Music and ambiences stereo `.ogg`; effects mono or stereo `.ogg`.
Music near -16 LUFS, peaks under -1 dBFS; effects normalised so they sit
together. `data/audio.json` maps ids to `{"file", "loop", "loop_start",
"loop_end", "volume"}` (loop points in seconds, when a track has an intro).

The score is orchestral with a synthetic edge (the Aether): strings, low
brass, piano, choir pad, timpani and taiko, a glassy bell/pad for Aetherite.
One short **friendship motif** (Cael and Rowan; a rising, unresolved phrase
of five or six notes) must return: hinted on a lone instrument in
`fortress`, carried by full strings in `last_light`, and on solo piano,
slower and broken off before its last note, in `remains`.

| Id | Length | Loop | What |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `title` | 40 s | yes | still, cold pad and a far bell |
| `war` (ambience) | 45 s | yes | distant explosions, clashing steel, shouts lost in wind, under a low unnatural hum |
| `fortress` | 70 s | yes | tense, determined march; the motif once, quietly |
| `battle` | 60 s | yes | fast, driving battle theme |
| `victory` | 6 s | no | short fanfare |
| `colossus` | 90 s | yes | the boss: heavy, mechanical ostinato, brass, choir |
| `overload` | 60 s | yes | dread: a pulse like a slow alarm under dissonant strings, rising |
| `last_light` | 150 s | no | starts almost silent (one held note, the motif on solo strings), grows through Rowan's farewell, and breaks into the full theme at 75 s; a climax at 120 s, then a single ringing tone that fades |
| `remains` | 110 s | no | solo piano and a few strings: the motif, slow, unfinished |
| `wind` (ambience) | 30 s | yes | cold wind over rubble |
| `hum` (ambience) | 20 s | yes | the crystal's low unnatural hum, beating slowly |

Effects (ids): `cursor`, `confirm`, `cancel`, `buzzer`, `text` (one soft
tick per letter of dialogue), `text_system` (a harder, digital tick), `step`
(boot on stone), `slash`, `slash_heavy`, `hit`, `hit_heavy`, `guard`,
`miss`, `spell_cast`, `ember` (fire spell), `frost`, `mend` (healing),
`ward`, `item`, `enemy_fall`, `atb_ready` (a small chime when a hero's gauge
fills), `encounter` (battle starts), `explosion_far`, `explosion_near`,
`rumble` (4 s, the chamber shaking), `collapse` (falling masonry),
`crystal_pulse` (a deep violet throb), `eyes_ignite`, `system_alert` (a
machine voice's chirp before it speaks), `colossus_step`, `colossus_slam`,
`colossus_sweep`, `aether_charge` (2 s rising), `aether_pulse` (the blast),
`resonance` (Rowan's strike: a shimmering rise, then a huge crystalline
impact), `overload_blast` (the crystal explodes with light), `klaxon`,
`unbound_1`, `unbound_2` (Rowan charging: unstable, each more violent),
`last_light` (8 s: everything rising into one white tone that cuts to
silence), `aether_wall` (Cael thrown back), `sword_fall` (a blade ringing on
stone), `star` (a single high twinkle).
